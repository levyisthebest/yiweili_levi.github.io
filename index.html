I am Yiwei Li (李轶为), a final-year CS Ph.D. candidate at the University of Georgia, where I am fortunate to be advised by Prof. Tianming Liu. I am also affiliated with CAMCA, Harvard Medical School, where I am fortunate to be mentored by Dr. Quanzheng Li and Dr. Xiang Li, and equally grateful to work closely with Dr. Lin Zhao (New Jersey Institute of Technology). I spent the summers of 2025 and 2026 as a Research Scientist Intern at United Imaging Intelligence (Boston), where I was mentored by Dr. Yikang Liu and Dr. Shanhui Sun. Earlier, I earned my B.S. from the Chien-Shiung Wu Honors College and my M.S. at Southeast University, the latter under the kind guidance of Prof. Hanchuan Peng on computational neuroscience. My work has received 3,900+ citations.

My research centers on multimodal large models (VLMs), video generation, world models, and reinforcement learning — building systems that perceive, reason, and act across modalities, with a strong pull toward AI4Med and AI4Sci.

"Bridge the gap between machine intelligence and the human brain — the essence of AGI will, ultimately, be multimodal."
